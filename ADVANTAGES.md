# Advantages of Rex Antorum Boz‑375 (Prospective 300 mm interceptor) versus PAC‑3 MSE (US missile defense standard)


## Mass

- Total weight:	315–325 kg Rex vs. 470–500 kg PAC

- Warhead:	10–15 kg Rex vs. ~8.4 kg PAC


## Performance

- Thrust‑to‑weight:	15.0–16.25	Rex vs. ≈13.0 PAC

- Speed:	Mach 4.7–5.3 Rex	vs. Mach ~4.5 PAC

- CEP (Circular Probable Error):	Hit‑to‑Kill optimized (0.1) Rex	vs. Hit‑to‑Kill baseline (0.3-0.4) PAC


## Cost/Price

- $250k (Production cost) Rex vs. $4-5m (Unit price) PAC

## Production Scaling Potential
- Rex Antorum Boz‑375: ~2640 units/year (conceptual capacity in 1.5 year)
- PAC‑3 MSE: ~600 units/year, planned increase to 2000 by 2030
