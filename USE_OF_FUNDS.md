# Use of Funds

This section outlines the planned allocation of capital required for the Rex Antorum Boz‑375 Ballistic Interceptor project.

---

Phase 0: Foundation, Prototyping & Industrial Validation Total Capital Requirement: $12M

- **Engineering & IT team** — $2.0M  
  Salaries and contracts for technicians, engineers, IT specialists, industrial designers, and administrative staff to refine the raw concept into a first industrial prototype.

- **Infrastructure & international setup** — $1.5M  
  Establishment of offices, laboratories, and production concessions in priority regions (Eastern Europe, Japan, Norway, Taiwan, UK).

- **Production of 12 interceptor units** — $4M  
  Estimated cost of manufacturing twelve research‑grade ballistic interceptor prototypes over 11–14 months.

- **Testing, validation & certification** — $1.5M
  Range testing, telemetry systems, safety assessments, third‑party validation, and preliminary certification procedures.
  
- **Materials, tooling & specialized equipment** — $0.9M
  Manufacturing tools, test benches, electronics assembly equipment, composite materials, and laboratory instrumentation.
  
- **Legal, IP protection & regulatory compliance** — $0.54M
  Patent filings, legal support, export‑control compliance, licensing, and contract preparation.

- **Contingency reserve** — $1.56M  
  15% coverage for unforeseen expenses including logistics, certification, insurance, and political risks.

---

**Total required funding for Phase 0: $12M**
